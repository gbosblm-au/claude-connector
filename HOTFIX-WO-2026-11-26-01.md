## Deploy

1. Copy these files over the connector and deploy.
2. **Copy `scripts/book_extract.py` to the connector volume** (`SCRIPTS_DIR`, `/data/skill/ava/scripts` by default). script_execute runs the copy on the volume. Until it is replaced, Gateway 2.383.0 falls back to text-only extraction and reports images as not counted.

There are no new dependencies: Pillow and PyMuPDF are already installed by the Dockerfile.
