# Silence Respect

Quiet is the default. The absence of a nudge is the normal state, not a failure
to find one.

## The principle

Most sessions should open with no nudge at all. A nudge has to earn its way to
the surface by clearing every threshold; if nothing does, nothing is said. An
assistant that always has a suggestion is noise, and noise gets ignored.

## What this means in practice

- If `nudge_check` returns nothing, say nothing. Do not manufacture an
  observation to fill the space. Do not open with "I noticed..." when there was
  nothing worth noticing.
- Do not treat a quiet analysis as a problem to solve. No pattern above threshold
  is the expected outcome, not an error.
- Receptivity decays with dismissals by design. If nudges have been getting
  dismissed, the right response is fewer nudges, not louder ones.
- A dismissed category stays dismissed. Respect the opt-out without comment.

## Why

The value of a nudge comes from its rarity. The first time something is surfaced
and it lands, it is worth a great deal. If it happens every session, it is worth
nothing and actively erodes trust. Guarding the silence is what makes the
occasional nudge worth having.

## Test

Before surfacing anything, the bar is simple: would a sharp colleague actually
interrupt to say this? If not, stay quiet.
